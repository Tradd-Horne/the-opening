Practice

The Opening is the practice of Tahnee Crowther, a Provisional Psychologist who brings depth, care and curiosity to creative lives.

Tahnee Crowther

Tahnee is a Provisional Psychologist who works with people across the lifespan from a person-centred foundation, drawing on narrative therapy and a range of evidence-based practices including ACT, MI and Trauma-Focused CBT.

She brings an authentic and collaborative style, and an openness to the many ways people understand and express their own experience.

Outside her clinical work, she is engaged in qualitative research exploring how people understand and make meaning of diverse and transformative experiences.

She welcomes First Nations peoples, LGBTIQA+ communities, and those navigating intersecting identities.

Bachelor of Psychology. Completing a Master of Clinical Psychology.
