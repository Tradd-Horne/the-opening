The Collective

Dr. Maren Voss

Clinical Psychologist & Visual Artist

Maren's practice sits at the intersection of clinical psychology and contemporary art. With over fifteen years of experience in trauma-informed care, she uses visual art processes to help clients access and process experiences that resist verbal articulation. Her own studio practice explores themes of memory, embodiment, and repair.

Eliot Marsh

Art Therapist & Sculptor

With a background in fine arts and psychotherapy, Eliot guides clients through three-dimensional creative processes that externalise internal experience. His work with adolescents and young adults draws on the physicality of sculpture and installation to build emotional literacy and resilience.

Dr. Lena Okoro

Registered Psychologist & Writer

Lena specialises in narrative therapy and creative writing as therapeutic tools. Her research examines how storytelling reshapes self-concept and builds meaning after adversity. She runs The Opening's writing groups and leads workshops on journaling, poetry, and personal essay as pathways to psychological insight.
