About Tahnee

Tahnee Crowther

Tahnee is a provisional psychologist who works with people across the lifespan from a person-centred foundation, drawing on narrative therapy and a range of evidence-based practices including ACT, MI, and Trauma-Focused CBT.

She brings an authentic and collaborative style, and has an openness to the many ways people understand and express their own experience. She has an interest in perinatal psychology and is available to connect with parent groups and community settings.

She welcomes First Nations peoples, LGBTIQA+ communities, and those navigating intersecting identities. Outside of her clinical work, she is engaged in qualitative research exploring how people understand and make meaning of diverse and transformative experiences.

Tahnee holds a Bachelor of Psychology and is completing a Master of Clinical Psychology.
