Mission

The Opening is rethinking how we care for the creative life, so that feeling and making can move together.

Position

Many of us are taught to keep our inner lives at a distance. We learn to quieten feelings, to work through exhaustion, to make things that look finished while something underneath stays unspoken.

For people who make things, that distance has a cost. The block, the loop, the work that will not come — these are often the places where something important is asking to be heard.

Approach

The Opening proposes a radical return to depth: slowing down, turning toward what has been set aside, and treating it as material rather than as a fault to be fixed.

The work is grounded in a person-centred foundation and draws on narrative therapy, ACT, MI and Trauma-Focused CBT where they help. It is unhurried, collaborative and curious.

Feeling×Making

The × asks for a radical realignment between the inner world and the made world, so that each can feed the other.

First principles

  1. Feeling as materialWhat we feel is not in the way of the work. It is often the work.
  2. Depth over speedReal change takes the time it takes. We make room for that.
  3. Curiosity over certaintyWe ask questions before we reach for answers.
  4. The whole personCreative life, work life and inner life are one system, not three.
  5. Many ways of knowingWe stay open to the many ways people understand and express their own experience.
  6. Rigour with playEvidence-based practice, held lightly enough for surprise.
  7. Meaning over symptomWe look for what a pattern is trying to say, not only how to stop it.
