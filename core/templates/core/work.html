Work

Two ways to work together: one-to-one psychotherapy, and consulting for creative groups.

01

Psychotherapy

For writers, artists, musicians, designers and anyone whose life runs on making.

One-to-one sessions that make space for the feelings and patterns that sit beneath the work: blocks that will not shift, the inner critic, reactions that feel bigger than the moment, and the experiences that have changed you.

  • Creative blocks
  • Patterns that repeat
  • Feelings held back
  • Identity & meaning
  • Transformative experiences
  • Trauma
02

Consulting

For studios, collectives, schools and organisations that make creative work.

Talks, workshops and reflective sessions on creativity and the inner life: how feeling shapes making, how groups can hold uncertainty, and how to care for the people who do the work.

  • Workshops
  • Talks
  • Reflective practice
  • Creative teams
